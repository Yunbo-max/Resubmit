Copyright/license rules: do not embed paywalled JCR full lists or redistribute restricted rankings. Verify journal metadata by ISSN or publisher journal identity; verify institution lists under authorized access. Do not infer a "closed" or "rolling" status from a bare date or general publisher page.

Researcher context: affiliation and verified institutional policy are decision inputs, not an assumption based on citizenship or current GPS location.
