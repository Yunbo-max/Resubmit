Newly written instructions, not copied runtime code. This skill has no mandatory
third-party dependency, model endpoint or dataset. See:
- https://github.com/lengmo1996/VibeResearch-skills/tree/main/skills/global/publish-venue-targeting
- https://github.com/Zsun79/ConferenceWatch

Adapted ideas: contribution-first fit, official-source checks, independent ranking
systems, full deadline stages and a revision-aware handoff. No claim of being first.
The author explicitly chose host-LLM prediction, not a Jev/Kev reconstruction or training
pipeline. We are not affiliated with ranking bodies or these upstream projects.
