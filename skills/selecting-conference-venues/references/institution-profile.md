# Institution and evaluation context — not nationality

**Do not decide publication criteria from a researcher's nationality, name, ethnicity or current IP address.** The relevant constraints are the institution and researcher's stated goal, school/department, journal/venue policy and institutionally accepted evaluation scheme.

## Profile inputs (recover if known; ask only when decision-critical)

- **Affiliation:** actual current and/or future institution, country, business school/department, evaluation context; multiple institutions when relevant.
- **Why publish:** field audience, PhD or graduation requirements, promotion/recruitment, grant/funder, publication incentives or UK REF-relevant institutional policy.
- **Thresholds:** which rank list, version and subject categories apply; hard vs preferred constraints; AND/OR where multiple criteria are required.
- **Time target:** conference full/abstract cutoff vs journal acceptance/publication target; distinguish "submitted by", "accepted by", "online by" and "indexed by".
- **Financial/policy constraints:** journal OA/APC, funder deposit/embargo, ethical/reporting requirements and prior publication rules.

When the institution is unknown but country is supplied, offer **possible local schemes to investigate**, not invented institutional requirements. If the publication aim is purely reaching the right scientific audience, no forced rank gate is needed.

| Context | Default source discovery, only if appropriate | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Mainland China, CS/engineering | Institution's actual directory; CCF for CS; historical official CAS journal partitions through 2025 if relevant; JCR/Scopus for journals | Calling third-party 2026 partitions official CAS |
| United Kingdom (UK) institution | Relevant indexing as context, funding/open-access policy, REF 2029 eligibility if institutionally material | Treating REF as a journal star ranking |
| Australian business school (Australia) | ABDC **2025**, institutional criteria; perhaps AJG/FT50 if requested | Claiming ABDC equals JCR quartiles |
| Business school anywhere | AJG **2024**, ABDC **2025**, FT50 **2026** membership and institutional acceptance; full paper vs editorial types | Assuming every business school uses FT50 |
| USA, Europe and other regions | Funders, departments, associations, appropriate indexes/discipline conventions; local policy only with evidence | Assuming a universal national ranking exists |
| Cross-border academic career | Show separate current vs target institution policies and trade-offs | Applying nationality as evaluation law |

## Example

A Chinese citizen currently working at a UK business school may request FT50/AJG reporting, UK institutional OA duties and future China faculty recruitment rules simultaneously. Present these as **three different evaluation contexts**; no one rule automatically overrides the others. Confirm policy edition and recognized submission or publication date.

## Handoff

Output profile includes institution, school, jurisdiction used for local time, evaluation objective, relevant scheme and version, verified policy URLs, current unknowns and research domain. Protect privacy: never export confidential staff/assessor documents or unpublished manuscripts into public venue queries.
