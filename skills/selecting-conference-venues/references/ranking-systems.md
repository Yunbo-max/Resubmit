# Ranking systems for journals and conferences

This is official-source routing guidance, NOT a bundled global ranking database or a promise of universal coverage. Ranking labels are independent; none measures the quality or probability of acceptance of an individual paper.

## Important 2026 changes

The CAS official updated journal partition publication series is **discontinued from 2026**; this changes what counts as a valid official citation.

- **CAS:** An institution may still use its own policy referencing a historical CAS partition; this does **not** create an official new 2026 CAS partition list. Never describe a third-party 2026 journal grade as the CAS National Science Library official update. Consult the official cessation notice.
- **FT50:** The Financial Times updated the FT50 set in April 2026. Do not silently use the 2016 membership set.
- **ABDC:** The official downloadable and searchable ABDC 2025 list supersedes old guides unless a target institution explicitly uses an older version.
- **JCR vs CAS:** JIF Q1 is a subject-category rank, **not** CAS 一区. AJG 4*, ABDC A* and FT50 membership are also different types of assertions.
- **REF:** UK REF does not give journals a universal REF 4-star grade for automatic individual-paper assessment.

## Verified entry format

Each journal: standardized title + print/eISSN + publisher, status for each checked scheme (verified/historical/not-listed/unknown/not-applicable), list year, category, grade/metric, official source, retrieval date and institutional acceptance if relevant.

Each conference: canonical series + edition + track + paper type/round, scheme year, source, retrieval date, verified or unknown. Never inherit a conference rank for all workshops and short-paper tracks.

Only apply a scheme as a **hard filter** when the author explicitly requested it or verified institution policy requires it; otherwise show relevant independent labels with caveats. When two schemes are explicitly required, preserve AND/OR. Missing data is not automatic failure or proof of omission. A licensed source needing an institutional login must not be bypassed, scraped or fabricated.

Never convert journal-level metrics into paper-level acceptance odds. Ratings aid routing; scientific scope, author requirements and revision feasibility decide recommendations.
