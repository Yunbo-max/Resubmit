# Journal and conference opportunities have different time semantics

Always identify the **specific publication opportunity** before making an "open now" claim. Use current official pages, retrieval date and applicable author policy.

## Conference (series + edition + track + round + paper type)

Verify abstract/mandatory registration, full manuscript, supplements, rebuttal/commitment rules, track scope, venue year vs submission year, time zone and extensions. A future meeting is not proof that manuscript submission remains open. Keep AoE/UTC-12 only when the official CFP says so.

## Journal regular manuscript (ISSN/eISSN + article type)

Many journals use **rolling regular submissions**, but each named journal's active portal, author guide, moratorium, invitation limitations and accepted article type require verification.

If confirmed, say **rolling — no general fixed manuscript deadline**, not a fabricated calendar cutoff. If access or acceptance rules cannot be confirmed, status is **unverified**.

Check word/figure limits, subject scope, double-blind policy, ethics, original/overlap rules, OA fees, licenses, funder obligations, cover letter and author instructions.

## Journal Special Issue / named collection

Key: journal ISSN + special issue call + article type/round. A special issue can have an opening date, deadline, invitation-only constraint, conference-participant requirement or special editor. Verify the official call, not the regular journal page alone.

A missed special-issue deadline **does not** automatically close the regular journal route. A journal accepting regular manuscripts **does not** imply its special issue remains open. For extended conference papers, verify substantial-new-content, copyright and concurrent-publication policies.

Publisher example: https://www.elsevier.com/en-gb/publishing/publish-in-a-journal/journal-selection/special-issues

## Distinct concepts of time

| Time type | What to verify | Never assume |
|---|---|---|
| **Submission eligibility** | Is the opportunity open, rolling, conditional, not open, closed, invitation-only or unverified? | Conference date equals manuscript deadline |
| **Editorial processing** | Publisher-reported metric and observation date, such as **first decision**, external review, acceptance or production time | A first-decision metric equals acceptance time or a guaranteed personal timeline |
| **Author career deadline** | Must the paper be **submitted, accepted, online-published, or indexed** by a school/funder date? | Rolling submission means it can be accepted before the user's deadline |

Do not invent a generic "2 months" review speed. A first editorial decision may be a desk rejection. If only historical metrics exist, label them as publisher-reported or historical and state their exact event/definition. For a journal without verified timing, return **time-to-publication: unknown**, not a confident date.

For conference candidates show the *earliest remaining mandatory action* and exact source time zone. For journal regular routes show verified rolling status, manuscript requirements and optional publisher turnaround data; for Special Issues show official submission opening/deadline. Only calculate timezone conversion when the source provides a precise instant. Do not replace unknown with zero.

Status labels may include open-verified, rolling-verified, conditional, not-open, closed, invitation-only, and unverified. Unknown sources must not be silently upgraded to "open".

Author choice and scientific suitability remain necessary even if an official portal permits submission.
